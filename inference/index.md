# Inference

<p class="intro">Where AI is heading and what that means for work and learning, argued from my own measurements rather than from announcements.</p>

## The series

In AI, inference is the moment a trained model produces an answer. In everyday usage, it is the conclusion one draws from evidence. This series aims for the second kind.

Each part starts from something I built or measured myself. Every load-bearing claim is checked against its primary source, and figures from my own system carry the date they were measured. The parts alternate between reports from practice and applications in finance and medicine; each stands on its own.
